Plumbline v1.0.0

A fast, zero-allocation spatial debugger and raycast visualizer for Roblox.

The Frustration

I built Plumbline out of sheer frustration. For a long time, every time I needed to debug a melee hitbox, check an NPC's line of sight, or calculate a bullet ricochet, I had to write a dozen lines of code to spawn a temporary translucent red part. Doing that 60 times a second tanked my framerate, bloated my memory with garbage collection, and made my workspace an absolute mess.

I just wanted a way to see my math in 3D space instantly without paying a massive physics cost.

Why Plumbline?

  • Zero-Allocation: Utilizes a recyclable HandleAdornment object pool. Draw hundreds of hitboxes every frame with zero garbage collection lag.
  • Bypasses Physics: Visuals are purely graphical. They don't wake up the Roblox physics engine.
  • Production-Safe: Flip Plumbline.Enabled = false in your live game, and all visualizer calls instantly bypass execution, costing zero performance.

API Reference

Vectors & Raycasts

Plumbline.Ray(origin, direction, result, [duration], [options])

Smart raycast visualizer. Automatically clamps the line to the impact point, draws an impact dot, and renders the surface normal vector. If the ray misses, it draws the full path in red.

Plumbline.Arrow(origin, vector, [color], [duration])

Draws a directional vector with an arrowhead. Perfect for visualizing velocities or custom normals.

Static Volumes

Plumbline.Box(cframe, size, [color], [duration])

Draws an oriented bounding box. Use this to visualize melee hitboxes or GetPartBoundsInBox.

Plumbline.Sphere(center, radius, [color], [duration])

Draws a radial volume. Ideal for explosions and GetPartBoundsInRadius queries.

Swept Shapecasts

Plumbline.Blockcast(cframe, size, direction, result, [duration], [options])

Visualizes a swept box projection. Shows the starting volume, the travel path, and the exact terminal impact box where the cast collided.

Plumbline.Spherecast(origin, radius, direction, result, [duration], [options])

Visualizes a swept sphere projection. Shows the starting sphere, travel line, and impact state.

Advanced Gizmos

Plumbline.Trajectory(origin, velocity, [gravity], [duration], [segments], [raycastParams])

Predicts and draws multi-bounce parabolic physics arcs. Calculates ricochets and elasticity for grenades, cannonballs, and physical projectiles.

Plumbline.Cone(origin, direction, angleDeg, range, [duration], [color])

Draws a 3D radar cone with depth rings. Essential for debugging NPC stealth detection angles, flashlights, and vision systems.

Plumbline.Axes(cframe, [size], [duration])

Spawns a professional XYZ coordinate gizmo with translucent glass planes. Use this to instantly understand the local space and orientation of rotating parts.
